## 质量管理的目标与原则

### L2-01-01 质量管理为什么重要：五点必记

Five reasons for quality management · PDF 3, 4, 5

- **五项重要性：改善患者安全、提升患者体验、减少成本与浪费、改善临床结果、提高效率。** Five benefits: patient safety, patient experience, lower costs and waste, better clinical outcomes, and higher efficiency.
- **改善患者安全：减少可避免伤害与医疗差错。** Improved Patient Safety: reduce avoidable harm and medical errors.
- **提升患者体验：理解患者反馈，改善接受医疗服务的体验。** Enhanced Patient Experience: understand feedback and improve the experience of receiving care.
- **减少成本与浪费：合理使用有限资源。** Reduced Costs and Waste: use limited resources responsibly.
- **改善临床结果：提升治疗成效。提高效率：改善时间与资源使用。** Better Clinical Outcomes: improve results of care. Increased Efficiency: improve the use of time and resources.
- **本讲详细讲 PDCA、5S、BPR、QFD；概览中的其他方法属于后续内容。** This lesson develops PDCA, 5S, BPR and QFD. Other methods in the overview are topics for later teaching.

### L2-01-02 质量管理的五项原则

Five management principles · PDF 15, 16

- **以服务对象为中心；持续改进；数据驱动决策；员工赋权；系统思维。** Customer focus; continuous improvement; data-driven decision making; employee empowerment; systems thinking.
- **服务对象包括患者、家属与服务提供者。改进应利用证据和反馈。** Customers include patients, families and providers. Improvement should use evidence and feedback.
- **让员工参与改进，并考虑部门与流程之间的相互影响。** Empower staff to contribute, and consider how departments and processes influence one another.
- **审核、反馈、纠正与预防措施、信息系统和领导支持有助于实施。** Audits, feedback, corrective/preventive actions, information systems and committed leadership support implementation.

## 质量评估框架与六项目标

### L2-02-01 结构：提供医疗服务的基础

Structure: resources for care · PDF 7, 8

- **结构（Structure）指支持医疗服务的物质、组织及人力资源。** Structure means the physical, organizational and human resources that support care.
- **例子：医院设施、设备、人员资质与培训、政策和财务资源。** Examples: hospital facilities, equipment, staff qualifications and training, policies and financial resources.
- **结构指标可描述某项操作中具备适当认证资质的人员比例。** A structural indicator can describe the proportion of appropriately accredited staff available for a procedure.

**辨析：** 口诀：Structure＝有什么；Process＝怎么做；Outcome＝结果怎样。This is a study aid, not a lecturer quotation.

**练习：** 列出 Donabedian 框架三部分，并举一个结构例子。 Name the three components of Donabedian’s framework and give one structural example.

**参考：** 结构、过程、结果。设备属于结构。 Structure, process and outcome. Equipment is a structural example.

### L2-02-02 过程：医疗服务如何执行

Process: actions during care · PDF 9

- **过程（Process）是医疗服务中的行动，包括沟通和临床操作。** Process is what staff and patients do during care, including communication and clinical procedures.
- **例子：医患沟通、给药规程、感染控制措施。** Examples: doctor-patient communication, medication administration protocols and infection control measures.
- **过程指标评估循证实践是否得到一致执行。** Process indicators assess whether evidence-based practices are followed consistently.

**课堂补充：** 老师强调：设备再好也不能替代良好的医患沟通；解释治疗可帮助患者理解与合作。Good equipment cannot replace communication that supports understanding and cooperation.

**参考：** 医患沟通和感染控制措施。 Doctor-patient communication and infection control measures.

### L2-02-03 结果：治疗产生什么影响

Outcome: results of care · PDF 10

- **结果（Outcome）反映医疗服务产生的影响：健康状况、康复与生存。** Outcome reflects the results of care: health status, recovery and survival.
- **例子：死亡率、患者满意度、疾病并发症、再入院率。** Examples: mortality, patient satisfaction, disease complications and hospital readmission rates.
- **结果可能受外部因素影响，因此单个结果指标未必直接等于医疗质量。** External factors may influence outcomes, so an outcome alone is not always a direct measure of care quality.

**课堂补充：** 老师以出院后因伤口问题或病情恶化再次住院说明 readmission；高再入院率应促使团队调查。Readmissions can prompt investigation of care problems.

**辨析：** 学习辨析：比较医院结果时，应考虑患者病情等差异；不要直接把高死亡率解释为差质量。课件的“易测量”不表示所有结果均容易可靠测量。

**参考：** 死亡率和再入院率。 Mortality rate and hospital readmission rate.

### L2-02-05 IOM 六项目标：全部要记

The six IOM aims · PDF 13, 14

- **安全、及时、有效、高效、公平、以患者为中心。** Safe, Timely, Effective, Efficient, Equitable, Patient-centered.
- **安全：避免照护造成伤害。及时：减少等待和有害延误。** Safe: avoid harm from care. Timely: reduce waiting and harmful delays.
- **有效：根据科学知识提供能让患者受益的服务。高效：避免资源浪费。** Effective: use scientific knowledge for people who can benefit. Efficient: avoid waste of resources.
- **公平：质量不应因个人或社会特征而不同。以患者为中心：尊重需求、偏好与价值观。** Equitable: quality should not vary because of personal or social characteristics. Patient-centered: respect needs, preferences and values.

**课堂补充：** 老师表示常在课堂练习中问六项目标，并特别补充 Patient-centered：虽然课件没有标红，它同样重要。All six matter, including patient-centered care, which was not highlighted in red.

**辨析：** 与 Lecture 1 区别：上一讲课件把 Timeliness and Efficiency 合在一个标题下；本讲 IOM 明确分为六项目标。

**练习：** 列出 IOM 六项目标。 List all six IOM aims.

**参考：** 安全；及时；有效；高效；公平；以患者为中心。 Safe; timely; effective; efficient; equitable; patient-centered.

### L2-02-06 有效性与效率：容易混淆

Effective versus efficient · PDF 14, 16

- **Effective 看循证服务是否达到有益健康结果；Efficient 看资源使用是否减少浪费。** Effective asks whether care achieves a useful health result based on evidence. Efficient asks whether resources are used without waste.
- **Timely 关注等待与延误；Equitable 关注质量公平；Patient-centered 关注个人需求与选择。** Timely concerns waiting and delays; equitable concerns fair quality; patient-centered concerns the individual’s needs and choices.

**参考：** 有效关注科学证据；高效关注避免浪费。 Effective concerns scientific evidence; efficient concerns avoiding waste.

## PDCA 持续改进循环

### L2-03-01 PDCA 全称与循环性质

PDCA overview · PDF 18, 19, 20, 21, 26

- **PDCA＝计划—执行—检查—行动，是持续改进的迭代反馈循环。** PDCA = Plan–Do–Check–Act. It is an iterative feedback cycle for continuous improvement.
- **Plan 确定问题与目标；Do 测试改变；Check 将证据与目标比较；Act 标准化或修订。** Plan defines the problem and goals; Do tests a change; Check compares evidence with goals; Act standardizes or revises.
- **课件将该循环与 Shewhart 和 Deming 联系起来；本节核心是理解它如何运作。** The slides associate the cycle with Shewhart and Deming. The key learning task is how the cycle works.

**课堂补充：** 老师反复强调没有固定“结束点”，改进后仍要持续检视。Improvement is ongoing rather than a one-off event.

**辨析：** Lecture 1 的 PDSA 使用 Study；本讲 PDCA 使用 Check。两者都是学习与改进循环，答题应按题目要求写全称。

**练习：** 依次写出 PDCA 全称。 Expand PDCA in order.

**参考：** 计划、执行、检查、行动。 Plan, Do, Check, Act.

### L2-03-02 Plan：问题、目标与方案

Plan · PDF 22

- **检视现有流程、找出问题、明确预期结果，并规划可管理的改变。** Review the current process, identify the problem, define the desired result and plan a manageable change.
- **目标清晰才能监测结果；从小改变开始，更容易评估。** A clear goal makes the result easier to monitor. Starting with a small change can make evaluation more manageable.

**练习：** Plan 阶段在设计改变之前，需要明确什么？ In Plan, what must you define before developing a change?

**参考：** 明确问题并设定目标。 Define the problem and set goals.

### L2-03-03 Do：小规模试行并收集数据

Do · PDF 23

- **小规模实施计划中的改变，并收集效果资料。** Implement the planned change on a small scale and collect information about its effect.
- **Pilot 是试点，不是立即全面推广。** A pilot is a trial, not immediate full-scale rollout.

**练习：** Do 阶段做哪两件事？ What two actions belong to Do?

**参考：** 小规模试点并收集数据。 Pilot the change on a small scale and collect data.

### L2-03-04 Check：对照目标分析结果

Check · PDF 24

- **分析试点数据，将结果与预设目标或以往表现比较。** Analyze data from the trial and compare results with the planned goals or previous performance.
- **图表可呈现多轮循环的变化规律，帮助判断哪些改变值得保留。** Charts can reveal patterns over repeated cycles and show which changes are promising.

**练习：** Check 阶段做什么？ What happens in Check?

**参考：** 分析数据，并将结果与目标比较。 Analyze the data and compare results with goals.

### L2-03-05 Act：采用、调整或保留原标准

Act · PDF 25, 26

- **根据发现，将有效改变标准化、修订计划，或在未证实改善时保留现有标准。** Use the findings to standardize an effective change, revise the plan, or retain the current standard if improvement is not demonstrated.
- **意外结果需要继续调查，必要时进入新一轮循环。** Unexpected results require further investigation and possibly another cycle.

**课堂补充：** 老师明确说：经过 PDCA 后决定保留现有标准，也可以是合理结果。Keeping the current standard can be a valid outcome.

## 业务流程再造 BPR

### L2-05-01 BPR 定义与四项原则

BPR definition and principles · PDF 40, 41, 42

- **BPR（业务流程再造）通过根本性重设计核心流程，提高效率与有效性。** Business Process Re-engineering (BPR) radically redesigns core processes to improve efficiency and effectiveness.
- **1. 围绕结果组织流程，而不只围绕单项任务。2. 由流程输出的使用者执行流程。** 1. Organize around outcomes, not tasks. 2. Let those who use the output perform the process.
- **3. 将信息处理融入产生信息的实际工作。4. 把分散资源视作集中资源来协调。** 3. Integrate information processing into the work that produces information. 4. Treat dispersed resources as if centralized.
- **学习比较：PDCA 常用于逐步测试改变；BPR 侧重整体性重设计。** Study comparison: PDCA often tests incremental changes; BPR focuses on substantial redesign.

**辨析：** 这里列出的是本课件第42页的四项原则，不冒充其他文献中更完整的原则清单。

**练习：** BPR 全称是什么？强调哪种重新设计？ What does BPR stand for, and what type of redesign does it emphasize?

**参考：** 业务流程再造；核心流程的根本性重新设计。 Business Process Re-engineering; radical redesign of core processes.

### L2-05-02 入院流程再造及风险

BPR in patient admission · PDF 43, 44

- **例子：通过集中式在线系统完成登记、保险核验及预约安排。** Example: one centralized online system for registration, insurance verification and appointment scheduling.
- **益处：减少文书工作、缩短等候、提高准确性。** Benefits: less paperwork, shorter waits and improved accuracy.
- **课堂风险讨论：患者隐私和数据泄露需要维护、更新及持续安全预算。** Classroom risk discussion: patient privacy and data leakage require maintenance, updates and an ongoing security budget.
- **跨部门共享病历有助于协调，减少反复寻找纸质记录。** Sharing records across departments can support coordination and reduce repeated searches for paper records.

**课堂补充：** 老师接受了“patient privacy / data leakage”的课堂回答，并强调安全更新属于需要持续预算的费用。The lecturer emphasized ongoing maintenance and security spending.

## 质量功能展开 QFD 与方法选择

### L2-06-01 QFD 与质量屋

QFD and the House of Quality · PDF 45, 46, 47

- **质量功能展开（QFD）把顾客表达及未表达的需求转化为设计要求。** Quality Function Deployment (QFD) translates spoken and unspoken customer needs into design requirements.
- **质量屋：用矩阵把顾客需要什么与机构如何回应联系起来。** House of Quality: a matrix relating what customers need to how the organization will respond.
- **VoC＝顾客之声（要什么）。VoB＝业务之声（如何做到）。** VoC = Voice of the Customer (Whats). VoB = Voice of the Business (Hows).
- **课件其他组成：关系矩阵、技术矩阵、技术相关性、规划矩阵。** Other slide components: interrelationship matrix, technical matrix, technical correlations and planning matrix.

**辨析：** 课堂转录有 VOP 等识别错误，按 PDF 第47页统一使用 VoB。质量屋不是建筑物，而是关系矩阵。

**练习：** 质量屋中的 VoC 和 VoB 分别是什么？ In the House of Quality, what do VoC and VoB mean?

**参考：** VoC：顾客之声；VoB：业务之声。 VoC: Voice of the Customer; VoB: Voice of the Business.

### L2-06-02 QFD 三目标与四步骤

QFD objectives and steps · PDF 48, 49

- **三目标：排定需求优先级、把需求转成技术特征、提供让顾客满意的优质产品或服务。** Three objectives: prioritize needs, translate needs into technical characteristics, and deliver a quality product or service that supports customer satisfaction.
- **四步骤：识别顾客要求；转化为技术规格；分析规格间关系；制定设计目标并排序。** Four steps: identify customer requirements; translate them into technical specifications; relate specifications; develop and prioritize design targets.
- **已表达需求可直接收集；未表达需求可能需要观察和恰当提问。** Spoken needs can be stated directly; unspoken needs may require observation and careful questioning.

**课堂补充：** 老师强调把抽象需求具体化，例如服务频率或等候时间，才能设计改进。The lecturer emphasized making requirements concrete.

**练习：** QFD 把顾客需求转化为什么？ What does QFD translate customer needs into?

**参考：** 技术特征与技术规格。 Technical characteristics and specifications.

### L2-06-03 康复服务：从需求到方案

QFD rehabilitation example · PDF 50

- **方便的预约时间 → 延长服务时段。** Convenient appointment times → extended service hours.
- **个性化治疗计划 → 个别化评估规程。** Personalized treatment plans → individualized assessment protocols.
- **清晰沟通 → 患者教育材料。** Clear communication → patient education materials.
- **延长服务时段需要人手与经费，设计方案必须可行。** Longer service hours require sufficient staffing and funding; the preferred design must be feasible.

**课堂补充：** 老师提醒延长服务时间会增加人手与成本，不能只写理想目标。Longer hours have staffing and cost implications.

**练习：** 康复服务例子用什么具体方案支持清晰沟通？ Which technical response in the rehabilitation example supports clear communication?

**参考：** 患者教育材料。 Patient education materials.

### L2-06-04 收集需求与决定优先级

Capturing needs and setting priorities · PDF 51

- **患者可能不会表达全部需求。结合反馈与观察，并按可用资源排列需求优先级。** Patients may not state all their needs. Use feedback and observation, and prioritize requirements against available resources.
- **课件提出如何用 QFD 设计患者门户，重点是需求、技术描述与优先级。** The slide asks how QFD could support a new patient portal; it emphasizes requirements, technical descriptors and prioritization.
- **学习例子：患者希望少漏诊预约 → 设计提醒，并检查送达情况及患者是否理解。** Study example: a patient wants fewer missed appointments → design reminders and measure successful delivery and user understanding.
- **领导支持、清晰方向和员工合作有助于落实设计。** Leadership, clear direction and staff cooperation help turn the design into practice.

**课堂补充：** 老师指出部分患者不愿表达意见，捕捉“顾客之声”可能有困难。Some patients may be reluctant to express their views.

**辨析：** 门户提醒例子为新增学习应用，并非课件报告的真实项目或研究结果。

**练习：** 为 QFD 改进排序时，管理者需要平衡哪两类因素？ What two categories must managers balance when prioritizing QFD improvements?

**参考：** 顾客需求与可用资源。 Customer requirements and available resources.

### L2-06-05 四种方法怎么选

Choose the right method · PDF 17, 26, 34, 41, 49

- **PDCA：反复测试与检视改变。5S：整理并持续维护工作场所。** PDCA: test and review changes repeatedly. 5S: organize and sustain the workplace.
- **BPR：重设计核心流程。QFD：把顾客需求转成设计规格。** BPR: redesign a core process. QFD: translate customer needs into design specifications.
- **学习综合：QFD 明确需求、BPR 重设计流程、5S 整理现场、PDCA 测试改进；不同方法可互相配合。** Study synthesis: use QFD to define requirements, BPR to redesign a process, 5S to organize the workplace and PDCA to test improvements. They can complement one another.

**辨析：** 这是跨章节总结与新增练习，不是教师原题。

**练习：** 哪种方法管理工作场所？哪种把顾客需求转成规格？ Which method organizes the workplace, and which translates customer needs into specifications?

**参考：** 5S 管理工作场所；QFD 把需求转成规格。 5S organizes the workplace; QFD translates needs into specifications.
