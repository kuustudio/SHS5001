## 田口方法与稳健设计

### L3-02-01 稳健设计的核心

Robust design · 学生PDF 8, 9, 10

核心重点 / Key points

Taguchi methods emphasize reducing unwanted variation during design so a product or process performs consistently under varying conditions.

田口方法强调在设计阶段减少不必要变异，使产品或流程在不同条件下仍保持稳定表现。

Robust does not mean that conditions never change; it means performance is less sensitive to those changes.

稳健不是条件永远不变，而是表现较少受条件变化影响。

The slides credit Japanese engineer and statistician Genichi Taguchi.

课件介绍该方法由日本工程师、统计学家 Genichi Taguchi 发展。

辨析与学习提示 / Study note重要辨析：不要把课堂中的“尽量减少变异”背成“完全没有变异”或“永远不用调整”。

### L3-02-03 正交表、参数与噪声因素

Orthogonal arrays and noise · 学生PDF 11, 13

核心重点 / Key points

Orthogonal arrays organize selected combinations of factors to study them efficiently. Signal-to-noise ratios help compare performance robustness.

正交表组织选定的因素组合，以较有效率地研究因素；信噪比帮助比较表现的稳健性。

The slides discuss dose, interval and biomarkers as factors, and patient differences or adherence as sources of variation.

课件讨论剂量、间隔、生物标志物等因素，以及患者差异或依从性等变异来源。

Noise means influences that are difficult to control in the design context, not necessarily audible sound.

Noise 在这里指设计情境中难以控制的影响，不一定是声音。

The chemotherapy example reports 35% fewer dose-related adverse events, but gives no identifiable study citation.

## 系统性风险评估

### L3-03-01 故障树分析的方向与逻辑门

Fault Tree Analysis · 学生PDF 16, 17, 18, 19

核心重点 / Key points

FTA = Fault Tree Analysis. Start from an undesired top event and trace combinations of contributing causes downward.

FTA＝故障树分析。从不希望发生的顶事件开始，向下追查促成原因及其组合。

FTA is top-down and deductive; basic events are linked through AND and OR logic gates.

FTA 是自上而下的演绎分析，以 AND、OR 逻辑门连接基本事件。

AND requires the linked conditions together; OR can be satisfied by any linked condition.

AND 要求相关条件同时成立；OR 表示任一相关条件成立即可。

The slide’s diagram uses an incorrect insulin dose as the top event and traces possible delivery or calculation failures.

课件图以错误胰岛素剂量为顶事件，追查可能的输送或计算失效。

辨析与学习提示 / Study noteFTA 从失效事件往下找原因，不是单纯按时间顺序画流程图。它可帮助根因调查，但不等于所有 Root Cause Analysis 方法。

### L3-03-03 FMEA 步骤与风险排序

Failure modes and effects · 学生PDF 21, 22

核心重点 / Key points

FMEA = Failure Mode and Effects Analysis. Identify how a process may fail, its effects and actions to reduce the risk.

FMEA＝失效模式与影响分析。识别流程可能怎样失效、有什么影响，以及如何降低风险。

Map the process, identify failure modes, assess effects and prioritize risks before selecting corrective actions.

绘制流程、识别失效模式、评估影响、排列风险优先级，再选择改进措施。

The medication case uses severity, occurrence and detection scores.

用药案例使用严重度、发生频度和可检测性评分。

Severity concerns consequences; occurrence concerns how often failure may happen; detection concerns finding it before harm.

严重度关注后果；发生频度关注发生可能性；可检测性关注能否在造成伤害前发现。

老师课堂补充 / Classroom commentary老师特别补充：流程图应覆盖所有步骤，不应只画已经知道的高风险步骤。Map all steps, not just previously identified high-risk steps.

辨析与学习提示 / Study note不同评分表对 detection 的方向定义可能不同，本讲材料未给评分表，不能自行补出统一阈值。

### L3-03-05 FTA 与 FMEA 怎么区分

FTA versus FMEA · 学生PDF 18, 21

核心重点 / Key points

FTA asks: what combinations of causes could lead to this failure event?

FTA 问：哪些原因组合可能导致这个失效事件？
