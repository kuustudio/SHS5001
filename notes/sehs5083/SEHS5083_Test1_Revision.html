Chapter 1 · P12 · Data → Information → Knowledge → Action

中文理解

数据是单独的观察值；信息把数据组织并加入背景；知识根据证据解释信息；行动把解释转成决定。老师备注用爽约说明：120人爽约→某社区爽约增加→交通可能是障碍→提供交通券及晚间门诊。知识阶段含判断与假设，仍需检验证据。

简单英文速记

Data are observations. Information adds context. Knowledge explains the pattern. Action uses the explanation to make a decision.

复习要点

  • 四阶段定义及顺序
  • 能把同一案例的四句话分类
  • 把可能解释与已证实因果分开

易错点：“交通可能是障碍”是解释，不能仅凭爽约人数确定。

原页文字

Data → Information → Knowledge → Action Data: individual observations Information: organized data with context Knowledge: interpretation using evidence Action: a decision or intervention

讲者备注（原文）

Example: “120 people missed appointments” is data. “Missed appointments increased in one neighborhood” is information. “Transportation may be a barrier” is an interpretation. “Offer transit vouchers and evening appointments” is action. Each step adds judgment and assumptions.

Chapter 1 · P13 · Health data beyond clinical records

中文理解

健康数据不只包含诊断和药物，还包括行为、个人体验、社会经济情况、环境、政策，以及服务可及性与质量。住房、食物、交通、污染及信任都可能影响健康，所以公共卫生问题常需多种来源。

简单英文速记

Health data include clinical care, behaviour, experience, social conditions, the environment and access to care.

复习要点

  • 列举非医疗健康数据
  • 说明社会条件如何影响健康

易错点：没有病历记录不代表没有健康需求。

原页文字

Health Data Is More Than Medical Data Health data can describe: Biology and clinical care Behaviors and experiences Social and economic conditions Environment, place, and policy Health-system access and quality

讲者备注（原文）

Explain that public health looks beyond diagnoses and prescriptions. Housing instability, food access, transportation, discrimination, pollution, insurance coverage, and trust in institutions can all influence health outcomes.

Chapter 1 · P14 · Unit of analysis

中文理解

先问每一行代表什么：一个人、一次就诊、一家医院、一个社区，或一天/事件。分析单位必须符合研究问题。某县的平均特征不能自动套到县内每个人；一个患者多次就诊也不能直接当成多个独立患者。

简单英文速记

The unit of analysis is what one row represents. Match it to the research question.

复习要点

  • 辨认人、就诊、机构和社区单位
  • 说明群体数据推论个人的限制

图表解读

原图是一条表格标题截图，重点是用列及每行内容判断记录单位。

易错点：把100次就诊误写成100名患者，或把社区平均值当成每个人的数值。

原页文字

Unit of Analysis: What Does One Row Represent? A row of data may represent: One person One clinic visit One hospital One neighborhood One day, event, or outbreak

讲者备注（原文）

The unit of analysis must match the question. If data are collected by county, we cannot automatically assume that every individual in that county has the same characteristics. This is a common interpretation mistake.

Chapter 1 · P16 · Quantitative data and numeric labels

中文理解

定量数据表达数量，包括次数、血压、身高、金额、等待时间、率与百分比。数字外观不保证它是数量：邮编、病人ID、病历号通常是标签，取平均没有实际意义。

简单英文速记

Quantitative data measure counts or amounts. A patient ID is a label even when it contains digits.

复习要点

  • 定量变量例子
  • 区分数量与数字编码

易错点：不要计算患者编号的平均数来描述健康。

原页文字

Quantitative Data Quantitative data use numbers to describe: Counts: number of clinic visits Measurements: blood pressure, height, weight Amounts: dollars spent, minutes waited Rates and percentages

讲者备注（原文）

Clarify that not every number is quantitative. A ZIP code, patient identification number, or medical-record number may contain digits, but those digits are labels—not quantities that should be averaged.

Chapter 1 · P17 · Discrete versus continuous

中文理解

离散数据是可数的整数，如0、1、2次住院；连续数据是测量尺度上的值，如37.2°C体温。记录经过四舍五入仍可来自连续变量；必须写清单位和精度。

简单英文速记

Discrete data are counts. Continuous data are measurements. Rounding a measurement does not turn it into a count.

复习要点

  • 次数与测量的差别
  • 四舍五入不改变原变量性质
  • 单位与精度

易错点：个人不可能有1.6次住院，但一组人的平均住院次数可以是1.6。

原页文字

Discrete and Continuous Data Discrete: countable whole numbers Example: 3 emergency visits Continuous: measured values on a scale Example: body temperature (e.g., 37.2, 39.3, 40.5) Units and precision matter

讲者备注（原文）

A person may have 0, 1, or 2 hospitalizations, but not 1.6 hospitalizations. Temperature can theoretically take many values within a range. Explain that data may be rounded, such as a weight recorded to the nearest pound.

Chapter 1 · P18 · Qualitative data

中文理解

定性数据包括访谈文字、感受、经历、焦点小组讨论和开放式答案。老师特别指出它可以科学而严谨：统一访谈流程、编码框架、归纳主题、多位复核者，以及记录结论如何得出。

简单英文速记

Qualitative data describe experiences and meaning. Clear coding and review can make the analysis rigorous.

复习要点

  • 定性数据例子
  • 解释定性研究如何保持严谨

易错点：文字资料不等于主观随意；把文字编码成数字也不自动产生可平均的数量。

原页文字

Qualitative Data Qualitative data capture: Words and stories Feelings and lived experiences Interview responses Focus-group discussions Open-ended survey answers

讲者备注（原文）

Emphasize that qualitative data are not “less scientific.” Qualitative methods can be systematic and rigorous. Researchers use structured interviews, coding frameworks, themes, multiple reviewers, and documentation of how conclusions were reached.

Chapter 1 · P19 · Nominal, ordinal and binary categories

中文理解

名义变量只有类别且没有自然顺序，如保险类型；有序变量有顺序，但级距不一定相等，如poor/fair/good/excellent；二元变量有两类，如yes/no。课件把primary/secondary/university作为school type的名义例子，但若表示教育阶段便存在顺序，应先说明变量的实际含义。

简单英文速记

Nominal categories have no natural order. Ordinal categories are ordered but gaps may differ. Binary data have two categories.

复习要点

  • 三种类别定义及例子
  • 有序不等于等距
  • 识别类别数字编码

易错点：原课件school type例子有歧义：教育阶段通常有序。答题用insurance type作为清晰的名义例子。

原页文字

Categorical Data: Labels and Groups Nominal: no natural order Example: insurance type, school type (primary, secondary, university) Ordinal: ordered categories Example: poor / fair / good / excellent Binary: two categories Example: yes / no

讲者备注（原文）

Explain that ordinal categories have an order, but the distance between categories is not necessarily equal. The difference between “poor” and “fair” may not be the same as the difference between “good” and “excellent.”

Chapter 1 · P20 · Mixed methods

中文理解

混合方法结合数量与意义：定量回答多少、频率、哪些群体；定性解释为什么、怎样和经历。疫苗记录可以显示某区接种率低，访谈再查交通、工作冲突、担忧或不信任。

简单英文速记

Use numbers to find the pattern and interviews to understand the reasons.

复习要点

  • 定量与定性各回答什么
  • 设计能互补的两种数据

易错点：一份含数字和文字的表格本身，不足以证明已进行严谨的混合方法研究。

原页文字

Mixed Methods: Numbers + Meaning Use quantitative data to ask: How many? How often? Which groups? Use qualitative data to ask: Why? How? What does the experience mean?

讲者备注（原文）

Use vaccination as an example. Registry data may show lower uptake in one area. Interviews can reveal whether residents face transportation barriers, fear immigration consequences, have work conflicts, or distrust healthcare institutions.

Chapter 1 · P29 · Electronic health record (EHR)

中文理解

EHR是随时间累积的电子健康记录，跨多次照护接触，供获授权者使用，兼有临床与行政数据。可包括诊断、药物、过敏、备注、检验、生命体征、影像与收费资料；首先用于照护，研究及公共卫生常是二次用途。

简单英文速记

An EHR is a digital health record built over time and used by authorised users across care encounters.

复习要点

  • 纵向、授权、多次接触
  • 临床与行政数据例子
  • 主要与二次用途

图表解读

配图展示电子健康记录的应用场景；定义与资料种类见幻灯片及讲者备注。

易错点：EHR并非为研究问题专门采集，因此不能假设所有字段都完整、一致。

原页文字

What Is an Electronic Health Record? An EHR is: A digital health record created over time Used across care encounters Accessible to authorized users A source of clinical and administrative data

讲者备注（原文）

Explain that an EHR can include diagnoses, medications, allergies, notes, laboratory results, vital signs, imaging, and billing-related information. It supports care delivery first; research and public-health uses are often secondary uses of data. Source footer: U.S. health IT guidance describes EHRs as longitudinal digital records containing information such as diagnoses, medications, notes, laboratory results, and other patient information accessible to authorized users. (healthit.gov)

Chapter 1 · P30 · EMR, EHR and PHR

中文理解

EMR通常围绕一个诊所或机构；EHR概念上支持不同照护场所的信息分享；PHR面向患者查阅和使用。现实中EMR/EHR用词可能混用，要根据本地使用、跨机构共享、患者使用三个重点解释。

简单英文速记

EMR usually serves one organisation. EHR supports sharing across settings. PHR is designed for patient use.

复习要点

  • 三者概念比较
  • 现实用词并非绝对界线

易错点：原幻灯片写HER属于拼写错误，本资料统一为EHR。

原页文字

EHR vs. EMR vs. Personal Health Record EMR (Electronic Medical Record): usually centered in one practice or organization HER (Electronic Health Record): intended to support information sharing across settings PHR (Personal Health Record): designed for patient access and use Real-world terminology is often inconsistent

讲者备注（原文）

Teach the conceptual distinction, not a rigid rule. In real organizations, people sometimes use EMR and EHR interchangeably. The important idea is whether the record is local, shareable across settings, and accessible to the patient. Source footer: HealthIT.gov distinguishes local electronic medical records, broader EHRs designed for information exchange, and personal health records designed for patient use. (healthit.gov)

Chapter 1 · P37 · Strengths and limits of EHR data

中文理解

EHR通常较及时，可追踪患者、协作照护、质量改进及公共卫生报告。它反映在该系统中曾求诊、获得照护、被记录且能取得的数据，不能自然代表全社区，尤其可能漏掉不就医或到别处就医的人。

简单英文速记

EHR data can support timely follow-up and quality improvement, but may miss people outside the care system.

复习要点

  • 列出EHR用途
  • 解释覆盖限制与代表性

易错点：记录很多也不能证明总体覆盖完整。

原页文字

Strengths of EHR Data Often timely Can follow patients over time Supports care coordination Useful for quality improvement Can support public-health reporting

讲者备注（原文）

EHRs can help identify trends, gaps in preventive care, medication safety issues, and service use. However, do not treat EHR data as a complete picture of the entire community. It reflects care that was sought, delivered, documented, and available in that system. Source footer: ONC notes that EHRs can support coordinated care, workflow improvement, patient information access, quality initiatives, and secure health information exchange. (healthit.gov)

Chapter 1 · P42 · Population → frame → sample → respondents

中文理解

目标总体是想了解的人；抽样框是用来接触人的名单或方法；样本是被选中的人；应答者是真正参与的人。只通过患者门户调查全县成年人，会漏掉没有门户权限的人，属于覆盖偏差风险。

简单英文速记

The population is the target group. The frame reaches people. The sample is selected. Respondents actually take part.

复习要点

  • 四个概念顺序与区别
  • 从患者门户案例找覆盖偏差

易错点：样本不等于应答者；抽样框不全时，增加回复量也未必消除偏差。

原页文字

Population, Sampling Frame, Sample, Respondents Target population: who you want to understand Sampling frame: list or method used to reach people Sample: people selected Respondents: people who actually participate

讲者备注（原文）

Use a simple example: If the target population is all adults in a county but the survey is sent only through a patient portal, the sampling frame excludes people without portal access. That creates a risk of coverage bias.

Chapter 1 · P43 · Better survey questions

中文理解

好问题应清晰、具体、中立、只问一个概念、有时间界限、用容易理解的语言。“你多久运动和健康饮食一次”同时问两件事，应拆开；“经常”需要界定。

简单英文速记

Ask one clear, neutral and time-bounded question at a time.

复习要点

  • 找双重问题、引导问题、含糊时间
  • 能改写调查题目

易错点：不能用一个答案同时准确表示运动频率和饮食习惯。

原页文字

Designing Better Survey Questions Good questions are: Clear and specific Neutral, not leading Focused on one idea Time-bounded Written in accessible language

讲者备注（原文）

Avoid double-barreled questions such as, “How often do you exercise and eat healthy foods?” A person may exercise often but not eat healthy foods. Avoid vague wording such as “regularly” unless you define it.

Chapter 1 · P45 · Survey strengths and bias

中文理解

问卷可快速、高效、匿名，便于用相同问题比较多人；但有不应答、回忆、社会期许和误解题目等问题。要问“谁没有回答”，不能只数有多少人回答。

简单英文速记

Surveys are efficient and comparable, but nonresponse and self-report bias can affect the results.

复习要点

  • 四种问卷限制
  • 区分不应答与回忆/社会期许
  • 大样本不必然代表总体

易错点：匿名可减轻某些顾虑，却不能保证消除所有偏差。

原页文字

Survey Strengths and Trade-Offs Surveys can be: Fast and efficient Anonymous Easy to compare across respondents But may have: Nonresponse bias Recall bias Social-desirability bias Misunderstood questions

讲者备注（原文）

Ask students: “Who may be least likely to answer?” This question is often more important than asking only how many people answered. A high number of responses does not automatically mean the sample represents the intended population. Source footer: CDC materials note that surveys can efficiently gather standardized information from many people, while also facing risks such as nonrepresentative samples, lower response, self-report limitations, and misunderstood questions. (stacks.cdc.gov)

Chapter 1 · P46 · Triangulation

中文理解

三角互证是结合不同来源增强解释：EHR看已记录病况与服务；问卷看体验和障碍；生命登记看出生死亡；社区资料看住房交通环境；访谈看意义。低筛查记录与交通困难访谈结合，可更好支持行动，但并非自动证明因果。

简单英文速记

Triangulation combines different sources to build a stronger interpretation.

复习要点

  • 选择互补资料来源
  • 说明资料之间如何支持或质疑同一解释

易错点：多个来源若共享同一种遗漏，仍可能一起出错。

原页文字

Triangulation: Use More Than One Source Combine sources when possible: EHR data: services and documented conditions Surveys: experiences and barriers Vital records: births and deaths Community data: housing, transit, environment Qualitative interviews: meaning and context

讲者备注（原文）

Triangulation means using multiple sources to build a stronger interpretation. If EHR data show low screening rates and interviews reveal transportation barriers, the two sources together provide a more useful basis for action.

Chapter 1 · P60 · Reminder attendance mini-case

中文理解

收到短信者出席率65%，未收到者50%，观察差是15个百分点。但两组可能在手机稳定性、交通、经济障碍上不同，不能据此断定提醒造成改善。随机推行、公平对照及考虑重要差异能提供更好证据。

简单英文速记

Attendance was 15 percentage points higher in the reminder group, but group differences may explain part of the gap.

复习要点

  • 65%−50%=15个百分点
  • 关联不等于已证实因果
  • 混杂因素与更公平比较

易错点：15个百分点不等于相对增加15%；也不要把比较结果直接写成因果结论。

原页文字

Mini-Case: Do Text Reminders Improve Attendance? A clinic finds: 65% of patients receiving reminders attended 50% of patients not receiving reminders attended Can we conclude reminders caused better attendance?

讲者备注（原文）

Ask students what else may differ. Patients who receive text reminders may have stable phone access, more reliable transportation, or fewer economic barriers. Better evidence might involve a randomized rollout, fair comparison groups, or analysis that accounts for important differences.

Chapter 1 · P63 · Law, policy and ethics

中文理解

法律规定必须满足的最低标准；政策是机构自己的规则和程序；伦理关注公平、权利、伤害、信任和问责。要同时问“可不可以做”和“应不应该做”；合法的数据用途仍可能污名化社区或分配不公。

简单英文速记

Law sets required standards. Policy gives organisational rules. Ethics asks whether a practice is fair and responsible.

复习要点

  • 三者定义
  • 说明合法不自动等于伦理妥当

易错点：不能用“符合法律”替代对公平、信任和伤害的讨论。

原页文字

Law, Policy, and Ethics Law: required minimum standards Policy: organizational rules and procedures Ethics: fairness, rights, harm, trust, and accountability Ask both: “Can we?” and “Should we?”

讲者备注（原文）

A data practice might be legal but still harmful or untrustworthy. For example, a technically lawful use of neighborhood data may still reinforce stigma if the community is excluded from decisions and receives no benefit.

Chapter 1 · P64 · Privacy, confidentiality and security

中文理解

隐私涉及个人对资料收集及分享的控制与期待；保密是受托者不随意披露资料的责任；安全是阻止未授权访问、遗失的措施，如密码、加密、权限、实体保护及日志。三个概念相关但层次不同。

简单英文速记

Privacy concerns control and expectations. Confidentiality is a duty to protect entrusted information. Security provides safeguards.

复习要点

  • 三个概念各配一个例子
  • 技术安全不能单独解决全部伦理问题

易错点：只写加密并未回答是否应收集或分享资料。

原页文字

Privacy, Confidentiality, and Security Privacy: a person’s control and expectations Confidentiality: duty not to disclose entrusted information Security: safeguards against unauthorized access or loss
