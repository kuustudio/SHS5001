## E1-49：采用障碍的简短回答

题目：Name two barriers to adoption and give two responses for each.

Resistance to change: Use open communication and staff involvement to help staff understand and accept changes. Resource constraints: Improve resource allocation and prioritisation with AI support. Use clear rules and human review to check AI suggestions.

变革阻力：通过开放沟通和员工参与，帮助员工理解和接受改变。资源限制：用 AI 辅助资源配置和优先级安排，并通过明确规则和人工审核检查 AI 建议。

核心关键词：**Open communication；Staff involvement；Resource allocation；Prioritisation**。

AI support、clear rules、human review 为补充应用例子，不作为本题必答评分点。若题目要求 critical reflection，可补充：**AI also needs resources and human review.**（AI 也需要资源和人工审核。）
